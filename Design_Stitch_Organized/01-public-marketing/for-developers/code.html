help Frequently Asked Questions

Developer inquiries answered.

How does Riyadah Elite protect our game from leaks? expand_more

Every playtester signs a legally binding NDA with biometric or verified digital signature verification. Furthermore, game sessions can be launched via our watermarked wrapper which injects invisible, unique steganographic identifiers into the render pipeline to trace unauthorized screenshots or video captures back to the specific tester.

Do we need to integrate an SDK into our codebase? expand_more

SDK integration is completely optional. You can distribute standalone private builds or issue Steam Beta keys. However, dropping our lightweight C++ / Blueprint SDK into Unreal Engine or Unity package into Unity provides automated frame-time hooks, heatmaps, and crash dumps without requiring manual log uploads.

How are playtesters compensated and motivated? expand_more

Testers are rewarded through our escrowed bounty framework and platform reputation progression. High-quality bug reports with verified reproduction steps earn developer-backed cash bounties, exclusive profile credentials, and early access to marquee tournament titles.

Can we test custom regional routing and latency? expand_more

Yes. You can isolate testers by ping threshold, specific ISP, and geographical regions including MENA, South Asia, Europe, and North America to evaluate tickrate stability and server mesh handshakes under real-world internet routing conditions.

How quickly can we launch a playtest campaign? expand_more

Once your developer organization is verified (typically under 2 hours), you can configure your cohort criteria, upload the build, and notify matching gamers in under 15 minutes.

Can we export bug reports directly into Jira or GitHub? expand_more

Yes. Riyadah Elite provides native webhook integrations with Jira, Linear, GitHub Issues, and Discord so that incoming bug reports, video replays, and crash dumps populate directly into your sprint backlog.
