About The Game

Cyber Strike is an adrenaline-fueled, precision 5v5 tactical first-person shooter constructed entirely on Unreal Engine 5. Blending hyper-responsive mechanical gunplay with modular cybernetic loadouts, matches hinge on squad-level macro strategy, precise recoil compensation, and zero-latency clutch executions.

Engineered specifically for low-overhead telemetry and esports-grade tick rates, this beta sprint tests our custom asynchronous rollback netcode across cross-continental routing hubs, ensuring absolute hit-registration fidelity under varied latency environments.

5-Step Testing Workflow

Pipeline Flow
01
Get Approved & Steam Key Delivery

Riyadah verifies your hardware telemetry against campaign demographics. Once accepted, your private testing beta key is deposited securely into your portal console.

02
Install Assigned Beta Build (0.8.4)

Redeem and pre-load the 42GB client on Steam. Configure the background telemetry client to log packet consistency and raw frame times directly to our sandbox.

03
Play Scheduled Playtests (2x 90min Sessions)

Queue during server load-stress windows on Friday & Saturday evenings. Engage in standard 5v5 unranked queues and dynamic game-mode stresses.

04
Log Bugs via Riyadah Telemetry Tool

Trigger Ctrl+F9 in-game to auto-capture state logs, memory dumps, and 30-second replay clips. Annotate reproducible glitches in under 90 seconds.

05
Earn Beta Badges & ₹1,500 Steam Voucher

Upon completion of your review rubric, escrow unlocks your cash/voucher credit directly to your Riyadah wallet along with an exclusive profile verification badge.
